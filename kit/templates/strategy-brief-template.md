# Technology strategy brief: <YYYY> Q<n>

An example template for the quarterly technology strategy brief. The future tech planner drafts it
from the radar, lifecycle reviews, POC outcomes, and the roadmap; the deck builder turns it into
slides. Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point `memory/context/future-tech.md` at the copy.
Delete the guidance lines in angle brackets when filling it in. It fits on five pages. Every claim
cites its source.
