# Team skills matrix

An example template for the team's skills, by role, against what the radar says is coming. The
training lead keeps it at `training/skills-matrix.md`. Shared views show roles and counts only,
never a named person's skills or gaps. Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point
`memory/context/training.md` at the copy. Delete the guidance lines in angle brackets.
