# Training kit: <topic>

An example template for a team training kit: everything needed to run one session on a topic,
usually right after a POC. The training lead fills it in; the deck builder makes the slides from
the session outline, through Kit. Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point
`memory/context/training.md` at the copy. Delete the guidance lines in angle brackets.
