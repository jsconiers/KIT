# Executive one-pager: <project>

Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point `memory/context/writing.md` at the copy. An example
template for a one-page executive summary of an impact assessment (IA) or a general design
specification (GDS), for leadership who will read one page and no more. `kit-onepager <document>`
drafts one from the document, citing every line to the section it came from; the deck builder
offers one alongside a deck. Delete the guidance lines in angle brackets when filling it in.

Use only what the document says. Where it says nothing, write "not stated" rather than filling the
gap; a gap leadership can see is better than a guess they can't. People appear by role, not by
name.
