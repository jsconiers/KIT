# Research brief: <question, as a question>

An example template for research briefs worth keeping in the library. It follows the order the
researcher reports in, so a brief can be filed as it comes back. Copy it, change it to suit the
team, and point the librarian or `memory/context/writing.md` at the copy. Delete the guidance
lines in angle brackets when filling it in. A brief fits on two pages.

| Field | Value |

| Overall confidence | <high, medium, or low, and why> |
| Valid until | <DD-Mmm-YYYY, or the event that would make it stale, such as a new major version> |
| Classification | <internal or confidential> |
