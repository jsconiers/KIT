# Lifecycle review: <technology>

An example template for reviewing a technology already in use. The future tech planner fills it in
and recommends a verdict; the decision belongs to the roles in `memory/context/future-tech.md`.
Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point the setup file at the copy. Delete the guidance
lines in angle brackets when filling it in. A review fits on two pages.
