# Learning plan: <person or role> for <technology or target role>

An example template for the training lead's learning plans. Copy it, change it to suit the team,
and point `memory/context/training.md` at the copy. Delete the guidance lines in angle brackets
when filling it in. A plan fits on two pages. Plans for one person stay with that person; anything
shared shows skills by role, never an individual's gaps.
