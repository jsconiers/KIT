# Stakeholder map: <project>

An example template for one project's stakeholders: who decides, who's affected, and who to keep
informed. Kit keeps one per project at `<library root>/artifacts/<project>/stakeholders.md`. The
status reporter uses it to pick audiences, and the scheduler uses it to plan who to invite and
brief. Copy it, change it to suit the team, and point `memory/context/status.md` at the copy.

People appear by role, not by name. A name, if your team wants one, belongs in the project's own
working notes, never in anything shared.
