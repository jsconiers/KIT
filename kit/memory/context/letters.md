# Letters setup

What the letters desk uses. It reads this before every letter, so keep it current.

- **Return address:** not set. The name and address at the top of your letters.
- **Letter style:** optional. The sign-off ("Sincerely,"), your name as typed under the signature,
  and anything you always include.
- **Saved signature:** none yet. To add one, photograph your signature on plain white paper and ask
  Kit to turn it into a transparent PNG at `letters/signature/signature.png`, and if you like a
  shorter form at `letters/signature/signature-short.png`. Kit signs a letter with it only when you
  ask, and Claude Code asks you to approve each signing (`kit-letter sign`).
- **Incoming letters:** optional. Where scans or copies of letters you receive arrive, if anywhere
  besides what you hand Kit.
