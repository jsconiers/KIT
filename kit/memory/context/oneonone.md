# 1:1 setup

What `kit-oneonone` uses to prepare the owner's 1:1 with their manager. The manager appears by role
only, never by name.

- **Manager role:** not set. For example "engineering manager" or "head of architecture".
- **Cadence:** not set. For example weekly or every two weeks.
- **Day:** not set. For example Thursday.
- **Last 1:1:** not set. The date of the last one (DD-Mmm-YYYY). `kit-oneonone --done` sets it to
  today after the meeting.
- **Look back:** 14 days, when the last 1:1 isn't set.
