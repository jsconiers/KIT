# Performance review setup

What `kit-selfeval` (your self-evaluation draft) and `kit-360` (feedback drafts for colleagues) use. Both
write drafts for you to edit; nothing is sent or entered into a review system.

- **Goals:** not set. Your goals for the period, separated by semicolons, each as "Name: words that show
  work toward it", for example "Reliability: outage, DR, resilience; Delivery: IA, GDS, design; Team:
  mentoring, training, onboarding". Finished work is grouped under the goal whose words it mentions.
- **Review period:** not set. For example "2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31". Without it the draft covers this
  calendar year to date (or give --from and --to).
- **Rating scale:** not set. For example "1 Below, 2 Meets some, 3 Meets, 4 Exceeds, 5 Outstanding". The
  draft leaves the rating for you to choose.
- **Word limit:** not set. The review form's limit on the self-evaluation, in words (for example 600).
  --words overrides it.
