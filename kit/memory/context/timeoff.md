# Time-off setup

What `kit-timeoff` uses to draft your time-off pack. It drafts only: the out-of-office message isn't set in
Outlook, no meeting is declined or forwarded, and nothing is sent.

- **Delegate:** not set. Who covers for you, by role or name (for example "the deputy architect"). --delegate
  overrides it.
- **Grace days:** 7. Items due this many days after you're back are listed as at risk too.
- **Urgent contact:** not set. How people reach someone for urgent matters (for example "the team's support
  channel"). Goes in the out-of-office drafts.
