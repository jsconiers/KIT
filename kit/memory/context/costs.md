# Cost setup

What the cost estimator uses. It reads this before every job, so keep it current. Keep prices and
rates here or in files it names: your copy of this file stays on your computer, and the repo only
has this blank template.

- **Rate cards and price lists:** not set. Each source, its date or version, and where it is.
- **Labor rates:** not set. Rates by role, and whether they're internal, contractor, or blended.
- **Currency:** not set. The currency every estimate is stated in.
- **Contingency:** not set. The rule, for example a percentage by estimate confidence.
- **Term:** optional. How many years to show recurring costs over. Three, unless you set another.
