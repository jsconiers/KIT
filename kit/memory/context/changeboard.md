# Change board setup

What the change board preparer uses. It reads this before every job, so keep it current.

- **Template:** not set. The change board's submission template, its sections in order, and where
  it is.
- **Risk scoring:** not set. The risk scoring matrix: its factors, their values, and how they add
  up to a risk level.
- **Submission checklist:** not set. What every submission must include, such as test evidence,
  rollback plans, and approvals.
- **Lead times:** not set. How far ahead of the meeting each kind of change must be submitted.
- **Cadence:** not set. When the change board meets, and any freeze periods.
- **Past submissions:** optional. Where earlier packs are kept, for questions the board tends to
  ask.
